About Harvard College

Founded in 1636, Harvard College is the oldest institution of higher learning in the United States. Our commitment to academic excellence, innovative research, and transformative education has made us a global leader in higher education.

With world-class faculty, cutting-edge facilities, and a vibrant campus community, Harvard College provides an unparalleled educational experience that prepares students for leadership in an ever-changing world.
